# Travel Demand, Planning & GIS Portfolio: Research Notes

## DOT·MPO practice drivers

The U.S. FHWA Metropolitan Planning Program fact sheet states that the IIJA requires USDOT to gather travel data and travel demand forecasts from a representative sample of states and MPOs, compare forecasts with observed data, and develop best practices. It also calls for opportunities to provide data improving the quality of plans, models, and forecasts. The same fact sheet highlights multimodal evaluation, Complete Streets, housing coordination, and safe and accessible transportation options as planning priorities.[1]

FHWA's GIS Open Data case studies describe how State DOTs and MPOs use open-data portals to integrate data across agencies, support performance management, make information accessible, and engage the public. The report cites standardized multi-stakeholder data management and targeted GIS applications as practical approaches for MPOs.[2]

FHWA's accessibility resource hub curates public resources including EPA Smart Location measures, Census Transportation Planning Products, LEHD/LODES, and HIFLD points of interest. It notes that EPA's products characterize built environment and transit accessibility at the census block-group scale.[3]

## Candidate authoritative inputs

| Input | Official source | Intended role in module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACS 5-year tract estimates | U.S. Census Bureau API | Resident workers, commute mode, travel time, income, race/ethnicity, poverty and vehicle availability indicators. |
| TIGER/Line / cartographic boundary geometries | U.S. Census Bureau | Tract-level mapping and spatial joins. |
| LEHD LODES | U.S. Census Bureau | Block-level residence-area and workplace-area employment; origin–destination flows. |
| EPA Smart Location Database | U.S. Environmental Protection Agency | Built environment, destination accessibility and transit-service context. |
| GTFS schedule feed | Transit agency open-data portal | Scenario travel-time / service access inputs where a feed is available. |
